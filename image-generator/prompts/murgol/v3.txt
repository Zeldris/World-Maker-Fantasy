Cinematic wildlife photograph, wide panoramic shot. Three Múrgol on vast red dust plains at sunset. The Múrgol is an enormous, massively heavy herbivore, wider than a bull, with a huge rounded hump and a body like a giant haystack of fur. Its coat is extremely long, coarse and tangled, hanging in thick matted dreadlocks almost to the ground, ash-grey with patches of rusty red where red dust has caked into it. All three have the same huge horns: they grow out sideways from the sides of the head and sweep outwards and forwards, much wider than the body, thick and gnarled like old driftwood, branching into several sharp tines, pale bone colour. The male in the centre stands close to the camera facing it head-on, shaggy fringe over a broad dark muzzle, black eyes with a faint yellow glint. The other two stand further back, one on each side, in side view with their heads lowered, grazing. Foreground of dry golden grass tufts on red earth in sharp focus, distant low mountains in haze, dramatic deep orange and amber sunset clouds filling the sky, low sun on the right horizon, warm golden rim light on the fur.
