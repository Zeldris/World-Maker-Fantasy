Cinematic wildlife photograph, wide panoramic shot. Three Múrgol on vast red dust plains at sunset. The Múrgol is an enormous bulky herbivore with a rounded, almost barrel-shaped body completely covered in a very long, dense, shaggy coat of dark charcoal-grey fur that hangs in heavy matted ropes down to its knees. The fur has a subtle reddish-brown tint where fine red dust has settled into it, never stripes. Its horns are huge and branching like the antlers of an old stag, with many sharp tines, made of pale weathered bone with a rough bark-like texture, spreading wide to both sides of the head. The male in the centre stands close to the camera facing it head-on, shaggy fringe over a broad dark muzzle, black eyes with a faint yellow glint. The other two stand further back, one on each side, in side view with their heads lowered, grazing. Foreground of dry golden grass tufts on red earth, distant low mountains in haze, dramatic deep orange and amber sunset clouds filling the sky, low sun on the right horizon, warm golden rim light on the fur.
