### 🎲 No new features until we've played everything

We've put Tarkor's roadmap on hold. The rule is simple: **no new tasks until everything already built has been tested by playing it**, from the first milestone to the current one.

- ✅ **135 of 139 tasks played.** The 4 still to go are in the living world (44 of 48 played); the rest of the game has been played through.
- 🔎 **185 findings confirmed by playing**: what went wrong, now fixed and seen working.
- 🔧 **45 fixed and waiting** to be played again, and **32 only partly fixed**.
- 📋 **40 findings still open** on the list.

Imagen: medios/jugarlo-todo.en.webp

---

**Why:** a new feature on top of something untested hides the bugs underneath. While this lasts, anything you see of Tarkor will be play-testing rounds and fixes, not game news.

**What's next:** play the last 4 tasks, clear the open list and then get back to the roadmap. Which part of the game would you like us to test most carefully? Tell us here.
