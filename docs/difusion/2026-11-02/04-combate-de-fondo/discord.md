## es

### ⚔️ Cuando no estás, la pelea pasa igual

En Tarkor hay **un solo motor de combate**, con dos formas de usarlo:

- 🎲 **En vivo:** si estás delante con tu grupo, la pelea es por turnos y con narración.
- 🌙 **De fondo:** si no estás, o si hay más peleas a la vez, se resuelve con el mismo motor, de forma determinista y sin narración.
- 🛡️ **Sin atajos:** nada de «N guerreros, 1d6 bajas» ni de «esta bestia vale 2 soldados». Un guardia, un soldado o un renegado pelean igual estés o no.

## en

### ⚔️ When you're away, the fight still happens

Tarkor has **a single combat engine**, with two ways to run it:

- 🎲 **Live:** if you're there with your party, the fight is turn-based and narrated.
- 🌙 **In the background:** if you're not, or if more fights happen at once, it's resolved with the same engine, deterministically and without narration.
- 🛡️ **No shortcuts:** no "N warriors, 1d6 losses" and no "this beast is worth 2 soldiers". A guard, a soldier or a renegade fights the same whether you're there or not.

**What's missing:** two fights are still resolved with aggregate numbers and have to move to the background engine. Until then, those two don't follow the rule.
