In the plan

From “leaves and returns after a dice roll” to “travels zone by zone along a real route”

What is being dropped: an abstract trip with a departure day, a return day and a risk rolled on arrival. On the map, a little flag between two nodes.
  1. It sets outExpeditions, caravans, hunting parties, patrols, refugees, messengers or visitors: anything that moves is a traveller.
  2. It follows a real routeZone by zone along the links between places and, inside each zone, across its cells. It advances every day and is where it is.
  3. You cross pathsYou can ask it about the regions it has crossed, trade, escort it, raid it or let it pass.
  4. It happens where it isA raid or a fight with wildlife takes place on the spot: live if you are there, in the background if not.
Factions use the same travellers and the same rules you do, like the AIs in X4. This is the plan, not something done yet.
