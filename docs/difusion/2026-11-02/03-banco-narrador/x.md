## en

**1/** We tested three newer models as Tarkor's local narrator against Llama 3.1 8B, the one we already had. Blind judging, in both orders. The old one won: 0.727 win rate. The fastest, Gemma 4 e2b, scored 0.500. #LocalAI
Imagen: medios/metricas.en.webp

**2/** All four models ran with the same prompt and temperature. Every pair of texts was judged twice, order flipped: the judge agreed with itself 0.939 of the time. If the winner changed when the order flipped, it counted as a tie.

**3/** Gemma 4 e2b cuts the turn from 29.3 s to 18.8 s (p50, on a Steam Deck) with the same RAM. But it loses two out of three head-to-heads against Llama 3.1. All three candidates were rejected.

**4/** Caveat: 11 situations, one prompt, and a judge that is another model. It's our bench, not a general ranking. The full write-up is here 👉 https://github.com/Zeldris/World-Maker-Fantasy
