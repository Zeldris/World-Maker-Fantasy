Wide cinematic panorama at eye level, layered depth from textured ground to hazy horizon, of an otherworldly landscape. A high, broken rock plateau at golden hour, seen at eye level from its edge, where the open steppe gives way to hot volcanic ground. Its red rock floor is split into great cracked slabs, and a deep jagged fissure runs from the lower right toward the centre-left, with thick white steam rising out of it. On the left, a cluster of tall, dark, tilted rock blades like giant broken shards of slate jut up at a steep angle, with steam curling at their feet; on the far right, only one lower broken rock fin. Low warm sun from the right through golden haze. The rock blades on the left are several times taller than a man; the slabs in the foreground are as big as carts; the fissure is as wide as a road and deeper than a house; the dry grass tufts reach a man's knee. A plateau of flat, cracked rock slabs like a dried lake bed turned to stone, their edges raised, broken and stepped. The fissure is ragged and uneven, wider in the foreground and narrowing as it winds away, its walls dark and layered. The rock blades lean in different directions, sharp-edged and flaking, in a loose uneven group. Small knots of rubble and low rock outcrops are scattered all the way to the horizon. In the foreground, big cracked red slabs, loose broken rock and clumps of straw-gold grass, with a wisp of steam from a crack. In the middle distance, the steaming fissure and a wide field of broken slabs and low dark rock outcrops, with thin plumes of steam drifting across. On the horizon, a flat endless red-gold steppe fading into warm haze, under a pale amber sky with thin bands of grey cloud. Rust-red and ochre rock slabs, darker brown-grey in the cracks; dark grey-brown rock blades with lighter weathered edges lit gold by the sun; white and pale grey steam; straw-gold grass, dry at the tips; red-gold far steppe; pale amber sky. A limited, muted palette of rust red, ochre and dark slate brown with one accent of golden amber. Every rock and spire sits in the same air and light as the land around it: soft contact shadows, drifting dust at their feet, and the farther ones fading softly into the haze. Everything is rough, weathered, dusty and irregular, never symmetrical or tidy. Style: photorealistic fantasy matte painting, cinematic concept art, soft golden-hour rim light, warm atmospheric haze, rich natural colour. Mood: epic, hazy, grounded. Shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens. The image is completely textless.
