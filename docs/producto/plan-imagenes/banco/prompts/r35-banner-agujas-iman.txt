Epic fantasy concept art: a richly detailed painterly matte painting of an otherworldly landscape, with cinematic realism, dramatic atmosphere and a touch of the mythical, wide panorama in layered depth. A wide desert plain at dusk, seen at eye level, with a dark crimson and purple stormy sky. In the centre stands a cluster of tall, black, sharply pointed rock pillars, like giant thorns, rising from a raised mound with a dark hollow at its foot. Around it, hundreds of small black rock cones are scattered over the red sand in wide rings, each with a few faint cold blue-green glints at its base. Large black rocks float in the air above the central cluster, with grains and pebbles drifting around them. On the right, jagged red mountains. Red sand combed into ripples. The central pillars are several times taller than a house; the floating rocks are as big as cars, smaller ones as big as fists, and countless grains drift around them; the scattered cones in the ring are as tall as a man. A central group of very tall, thin, sharply pointed black rock pillars like giant thorns, the tallest almost needle-thin, standing on a raised mound of dark rock with a rounded hollow in its side. Around it, scattered in wide rings over the sand, hundreds of smaller black rock cones, jagged and rough like broken tooth stumps. Large angular black rocks hang motionless in the air at different heights above the cluster, each as big as a car, with grains and pebbles drifting near them. Sand combed into long ripples between the cones. In the foreground, rippled deep red sand with scattered black cones and blue-green glints. In the middle distance, the central cluster with floating rocks above it. On the right, jagged red mountains; on the left, a low distant range under a glowing pink horizon. The sky is dark crimson and purple, with dark storm clouds toward the right top. Matte black rock, rough and grainy, with dark red-brown edges where lit; deep red-brown sand; small cold blue-green glints at the base of the cones; red-orange mountains; crimson, dark purple and black sky with a pink glow at the horizon. A limited, muted palette of black, deep crimson and dark purple with one accent of cold blue-green. Nothing is symmetrical, tidy or clean: every part, plate, rock and tuft is irregular, uneven, weathered and chipped, each a different size and shape. The light is soft and hazy through dust in the air, with deep shadows, muted contrast and no bright highlights. The image is completely textless.
