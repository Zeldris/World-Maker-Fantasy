Epic fantasy concept art: a richly detailed painterly matte painting with cinematic realism, dramatic atmosphere and a touch of the mythical, of an invented creature that does not exist in nature. A single huge heavy armoured reptile, full body in three-quarter view walking toward the left of the camera, centre of the frame, head low. Low warm sun from the left. It has the body of a rhinoceros the size of a house, with four thick pillar legs and wrinkled elephant-like feet. Rough pale bone plates, like a stack of broken tiles, rise on its back as a jagged crest and cover its head as a wide shield. Three long horns like a buffalo's, curving up, sprout from its snout and jaw. Its dark grey flank is ribbed like corrugated sheet. Two more of the same walk beside it. Cracked red earth, red cliffs. As tall and as long as a two-storey house; each leg as thick as an old tree trunk; far bigger than the two others beside it, which are about two thirds its size, and than the herd on the horizon. The body of a rhinoceros with a barrel of ribs and four thick pillar legs, ending in broad round feet with wrinkled skin like an elephant's. The flank is ribbed with deep parallel grooves, like corrugated metal sheet. A jagged crest of large pale bone slabs and spikes, like broken roof tiles set on edge, runs along the back and shoulders, and a wide spiked bone shield covers the top of the head and neck. A heavy head carried low, with a deep mouth showing teeth. Three long horns that sweep up and forward like a buffalo's: one on each side of the jaw and one longer one on the snout. In the foreground, red clay earth cracked like a dry lake bed. In the middle distance, a haze of orange dust at the feet of the beasts and a flat plain. On the right, a tall red sandstone cliff, and on the horizon a distant herd of more of the same, tiny, under a golden sky with thin clouds and the low sun on the left. Dark grey-brown wrinkled hide, dusty; pale beige-cream bone plates, stained darker brown in their cracks and chipped at the tips; pale ivory horns tipped with dark grey; red-brown cracked earth; orange dust; red cliff. Beside it, two more of the same beasts, about two thirds its size, walking the same way; far behind, a herd of many more, tiny and hazy on the horizon. A limited, muted palette of bone beige, dark grey-brown and rust red with one accent of golden orange. Nothing is symmetrical, tidy or clean: every part, plate, rock and tuft is irregular, uneven, weathered and chipped, each a different size and shape. The light is soft and hazy through dust in the air, with deep shadows, muted contrast and no bright highlights. The image is completely textless.
