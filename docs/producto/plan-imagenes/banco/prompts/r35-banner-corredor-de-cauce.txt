Epic fantasy concept art: a richly detailed painterly matte painting with cinematic realism, dramatic atmosphere and a touch of the mythical, of an invented creature that does not exist in nature. A single lean six-legged runner, full body in side view facing left, leaping across the centre of the frame with all feet off the ground. Hazy warm daylight from the upper right. It has the long lizard-like body of a greyhound, an otter's head with a long muzzle and long pale whiskers, and tight leathery rust-brown skin like a rhino's. Overlapping brown plates, like an armadillo's bands, cover its back from neck to tail. Its long whip-thin tail ends in a point. Two forelegs fold under its chest and four long hind legs stretch back. Cracked red earth, a cloud of dust, a blurred herd running behind. Longer than a horse from chest to tail root, with a tail as long as its whole body; its feet are well above the cracked earth in the leap, and the herd behind is much smaller with distance. A long, low lizard-like body with the deep chest and narrow waist of a greyhound. A neck held up and forward, a small head like an otter's with a long blunt muzzle, round dark eyes, small round ears set close to the skull, and two small curved horns pointing back from the top of the head. Long pale whiskers sweep back from the muzzle like a catfish's. Along the back runs a row of broad overlapping plates, like the bands of an armadillo, from the neck over the haunches and onto the tail, which is long, thin and whip-like and ends in a sharp point. Two forelegs are folded up against the chest with clawed feet, and four long hind legs in two pairs stretch backwards, each ending in a clawed foot. In the foreground, red clay earth cracked like a dry lake bed, scattered with small dark stones. In the middle distance, a thick cloud of orange dust kicked up on the left and a trail of dust behind its feet on the right. On the horizon, low flat-topped hills fading in the haze under a cloudy amber sky. Rust-brown leathery skin with darker brown folds at the joints; brown bone plates with paler edges; pale grey whiskers; dark brown eyes; red-brown cracked earth; orange-brown dust. Behind it, eight or ten more of the same runners at different distances, running the same way in a haze of dust, small, blurred and pale with distance. A limited, muted palette of rust brown, burnt orange and dusty tan with one accent of pale cream. Nothing is symmetrical, tidy or clean: every part, plate, rock and tuft is irregular, uneven, weathered and chipped, each a different size and shape. The light is soft and hazy through dust in the air, with deep shadows, muted contrast and no bright highlights. The image is completely textless.
