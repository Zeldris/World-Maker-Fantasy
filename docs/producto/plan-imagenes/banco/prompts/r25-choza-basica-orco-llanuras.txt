Wide cinematic panorama from a low camera angle, layered depth from textured ground to hazy horizon, subject fully in frame. A single domed hut standing alone on a red desert plain, seen from a low camera angle three-quarters from the front, filling the left half of the image. The dome is about twice as wide as it is tall, and its low entrance reaches only a third of its height. A single low, dark doorway opens at the front, framed by a thick upright bone post carved with a bold black clan mark. The hides are weathered and faded where the wind strikes them, and red dust has piled in a low drift along the windward side. Its frame is a ring of huge, curved pale bones driven into the ground and bent together over the top, lashed at every crossing with thick cords of dark tendon. Sewn hides in patched shades of red-brown and tan are stretched tight between the bones as walls and roof, their seams stitched with coarse cord. A dry wind tugs at the loose edge of a hide near the base and blows a thin streamer of red dust past the doorway. The ground is cracked red earth with sparse dry tufts; to the left, tall red rock spires rise in the middle distance, and flat-topped mesas fade into haze on the far horizon under a wide sky of broken golden cloud. Warm, hazy daylight falls from high on the right, picking out the curve of the bones and the stitched seams, and laying a soft shadow to the left of the hut. Style: photorealistic fantasy matte painting, cinematic concept art, soft golden-hour rim light, warm atmospheric haze, rich natural colour. Mood: epic, hazy, grounded.
