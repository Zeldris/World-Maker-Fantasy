Epic fantasy concept art: a richly detailed painterly matte painting with cinematic realism, dramatic atmosphere and a touch of the mythical, of an invented creature that does not exist in nature. A single tall male herd grazer, full body in three-quarter view, centre of the frame, head turned to the viewer. Hazy daylight from the left. Two wide, flat horns shaped like broad leaf blades, pale bone grey and veined like real leaves, stick out straight sideways from the top of its head. It has the small head of a deer on the upright neck of a giraffe, as long as its legs, and long thin legs ending in big splayed three-toed feet like a bird's. Very short rust-red coat, cream white underneath and on the front of the neck. Five more graze behind it. Red desert earth, dry scrub, red mesas. From the shoulder to the top of the head it measures the same as from the shoulder to the ground, so the head is held higher than a giraffe's, about three and a half metres up; the horns spread wider than its shoulders; it is far taller than the scrub around its feet. A tall, slender body like a gazelle's on very long thin legs, each ending in a large splayed foot of three clawed toes like a big bird's. A long upright neck, thick at the base and tapering toward the head, as long as the legs. A small head like a deer's, with large dark eyes and a short tuft of beard. Two wide, flat, leaf-shaped horns, like broad willow leaves with a pointed tip and a central vein, sticking out straight sideways in a line from the top of the head, each as long as the head is. In the foreground, red desert earth with dry scrub, tufts of pale grass and a few red rocks. In the middle distance, open dry steppe with the herd. On the horizon, flat-topped red sandstone mesas and a rock tower, under a blue sky with scattered white clouds. Very short rust-red coat along the back and neck, dusty at the legs; cream white underneath, the front of the neck and the inner legs; pale bone-grey leaf horns with darker veins; grey feet; red clay earth; straw-coloured grass; dark green scrub. Around it graze and wander five more of the same animals at different distances, each tall and slender with a long upright neck, a small head and two flat pale leaf-blade horns, smaller and softer with distance. A limited, muted palette of rust red, cream white and straw yellow with one accent of pale blue sky. Nothing is symmetrical, tidy or clean: every part, plate, rock and tuft is irregular, uneven, weathered and chipped, each a different size and shape. The light is soft and hazy through dust in the air, with deep shadows, muted contrast and no bright highlights. The image is completely textless.
